## Which model

Late October 2026. Pick for the job, not a forever ranking.

| Job | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Speech, sound, realism, short takes | Veo 3.1 |
| Longer clip, several shots, up to ~30s | Seedance 2.5 or Kling |
| Cheap draft | Wan 3.0 |

Skip Sora. The app shut down in April 2026. The API shut down in September 2026.

Prompt shape that breaks less often: frame, who is in it, one action, camera, light, style, sound.

## 1. Vertical UGC

Seedance or Kling. 9:16. 5–8 seconds.

```
9:16 handheld phone shot in a sunlit kitchen. A person lifts a matte ceramic cup, takes one sip, then looks into the lens and gives a small real smile. Slow push-in. Window light, shallow depth of field, realistic skin, no beauty filter, no logos, no captions. Room tone. One short line, casual: okay, this is actually good.
```

## 2. Road and fog

Veo 3.1. 16:9.

```
Wide 16:9. Empty coastal road at blue hour, one car drives toward camera as sea fog crosses the wet asphalt. Low drone, gentle rise. Cold cyan and amber taillights, anamorphic flares, photoreal, no text, no logos. Wind and distant surf.
```

## 3. A product in three shots

Kling. 9:16.

```
9:16, three shots in one clip. Shot 1: macro of a plain silver ring on black stone, a light sweep. Shot 2: a hand picks it up. Shot 3: the ring on a finger against a night city window. Hard key light, luxury commercial, crisp metal, no logos, no text, no music lyrics.
```

## 4. Night street, prompt of the day

Seedance 2.5 or Kling. 9:16. 6–8 seconds, with sound.

```
9:16 handheld phone video, slight natural shake. A person in a wool coat stands on a wet night street, opens a paper cup of coffee, steam catches a neon sign. They glance at the lens once, no smile, then look away. Slow creep-in. Practical neon, cyan and amber, realistic skin, no beauty filter, no logos, no captions. Ambient city noise, one sip, paper cup crinkle.
```

If the face melts, drop the second camera move. One action. No text in the frame.
